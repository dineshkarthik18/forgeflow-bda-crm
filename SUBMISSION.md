# Technical Assessment Submission Notes

## Project Choice

I chose the **Business Development Associate Team Module for a Manufacturing Company**.

## Why This Module

This module gives the best opportunity to show practical MERN skills: React dashboard design, Express APIs, MongoDB schemas, business workflow thinking, validation, and realistic CRM operations. It is also easy to explain in an interview because every feature maps to a real BDA task.

## Demo Script

1. Open the dashboard and explain the manufacturing sales context.
2. Show the top metrics: open pipeline, weighted forecast, due follow-ups, and win rate.
3. Filter leads by stage and search for a company or product.
4. Open a lead card and explain priority, owner, probability, next action, and communication options.
5. Show team performance progress for target tracking.
6. Open the Express API health endpoint and lead endpoint to demonstrate backend readiness.

## Future Enhancements

- Role-based login for Admin, BDA, and Manager.
- Drag-and-drop lead movement between stages.
- Production handoff workflow after a deal is marked Won.
- Email/WhatsApp reminders for due follow-ups.
- Charts for monthly territory performance and lead source ROI.
